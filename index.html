What it does

🎯 Biggest bank for buck

Ranks all 309 AR-DRG v9.0 groups by the money at stake on insured cases at the minor→major coding boundary — including what a shift of just 1% of cases is worth.

🏥 Facility targeting

Import a patient-level DARS extract (CSV/Excel). The tool computes each facility's casemix index, flags those above/below the international target, and ranks facilities by realisable SAR opportunity.

📊 Chapters & benchmarks

Browse DRG groups by clinical chapter with their MINC/MAJC prices and the evidenced international minor/major casemix split for each group.

⚙️ Simulator

Model the revenue effect of reclassification programmes — shift a % of Minor episodes to Major across all DRGs, high-risk DRGs, or the top 20.

How the opportunity is calculated

Facility minor share − international benchmark minor share
× cases × minor→major uplift (SAR)
× insured share of the DRG (claims ÷ DARS activity)

Only insured cases can justifiably generate additional revenue, so every opportunity is weighted by the insured share of activity from the CHI price table.

Benchmark evidence

Australia — AIHW AR-DRG data cubes PRIMARY SOURCE
Free public separations per AR-DRG code. The KSA DRG model is an AR-DRG v9.0 adaptation, so complexity-tier splits for 306 of 309 DRG groups are computed directly from the official v9.0 cube (2019–20) — the same classification version as the CHI price table.
United Kingdom — NHS England Hospital Admitted Patient Care Activity SECONDARY
Public episode counts per HRG4+ code and unit costs; useful at chapter level.
Germany — BfArM G-DRG report browser SECONDARY
Public case counts per G-DRG including complexity splits; useful for spot-checking families.
GCC — UAE DoH (Shafafiya) & KSA CHI NOT PUBLICLY PUBLISHED
Regulators publish tariff structures but not casemix activity shares per tier. Recommend requesting tier splits from CHI/DARS; until then the tool uses the Australian evidence as the external anchor.
